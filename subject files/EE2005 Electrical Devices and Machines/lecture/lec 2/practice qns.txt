Question 1.

Consider a current of 3 A flowing through a coil of 100 turns in a magnetic circuit. Find the mmf in the magnetic circuit.  

Answer: 300 At


Question 2.

A coil of 200 turns is wound on a magnetic core. The magnetic core has a reluctance of 400000 H-1. If a current of 2 A is flowing through the coil, find the magnetic flux in the magnetic core. 

Answer: 1 mWb


Question 3.

The mean length of the magnetic core is 20 cm, the cross section is 5 cm2, and the relative permeability of the core material is 3000. Find the reluctance of the magnetic core.


Answer: 106103 H-1
