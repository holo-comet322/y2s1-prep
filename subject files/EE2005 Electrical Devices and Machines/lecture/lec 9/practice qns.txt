Question 1.

A 75 kVA, 4800/240 V, 50 Hz, single-phase transformer delivers one-half of its rated load at 0.96 lagging power factor at rated voltage. Using the approximate equivalent circuit referred to the high-voltage side, determine:

(a) The magnitude of the load voltage referred to the high-voltage side,

(b) The magnitude of the load current referred to the high-voltage side, and

(c) The magnitude of the input voltage.

The parameters of the transformer equivalent circuit are :

R1 = 2.488 Ω, X1 = 4.8384 Ω, Rc1 = 44.202 kΩ, Xm1 = 7.7986 kΩ

R2 = 0.006 Ω, X2 = 0.0121 Ω

Answers:

(a) 4800 V

(b) 7.813 A

(c) 4858 V
