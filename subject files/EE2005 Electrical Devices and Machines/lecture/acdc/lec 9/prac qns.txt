Question 1.

A 240 V DC shunt motor draws an input current of 37.5 A when running at the rated speed of 2500 rpm. The motor parameters are RA= 0.37 Ω, which includes the armature, interpoles and compensating windings resistances, and RF = 160 Ω. A rheostat is inserted in the field circuit which reduces the flux to 75 % of its rated value and a 1 ohm resistor is also added in series with the armature. Assume that the resultant load torque is 50 % of its value at rated conditions and the machine is operating in the linear part of the magnetisation curve. Neglect rotational losses.

Determine:

(a) The armature current

(b) The steady-state speed

Answers:

(a) 24 A

(b) 3046 rpm
