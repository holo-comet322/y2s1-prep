Question 1.

The rotor of a 6-pole, 60 Hz induction motor has a per-phase equivalent resistance and blocked-rotor reactance of RR = 0.1 Ω and XRO = 0.54 Ω, respectively. The blocked-rotor induced voltage per phase ERO = 150 V. If the rotor is running at 1164 rpm, determine the following:

(a) Synchronous speed

(b) Slip

(c) Rotor current

(d) Rotor current if changing the shaft load resulted in 1.24 % slip

(e) Rotor speed for the condition in (d)

Answers:

(a) 1200 rpm

(b) 0.03

(c) 44.4Ð-9.20° A

(d) 18.6Ð-3.83° A

(e) 1185 rpm

Question 2. 

A 460 V, 50 Hz, 2-pole induction motor has an output power of 50 kW while running at a slip of 5 %. The rotational losses are 1300 W. Determine the following: 

(a) Shaft speed

(b) Load torque

(c) Induced or developed torque

(d) Rotor frequency

Answers:

(a) 2850 rpm

(b) 167.5 Nm

(c) 171.9 Nm

(d) 2.5 Hz
