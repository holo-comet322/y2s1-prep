Question 1.

A 3-phase, 460 V, 50 hp, 60 Hz, 4-pole induction motor runs at 1755 rpm. What is the slip?

Answer: 2.5 %


Question 2.

A 3-phase, 460 V, 50 hp, 60 Hz, 4-pole induction motor runs at a slip of 2.5 %. Determine the speed of the motor. 

Answer: 1755 rpm


Question 3.

The synchronous speed of a 3-phase, 460 V, 50 hp, 60 Hz, induction motor is 1800 rpm. Determine the number of poles.

Answer: 4
