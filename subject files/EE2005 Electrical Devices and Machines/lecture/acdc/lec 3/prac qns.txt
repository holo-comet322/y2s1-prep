Question 1.

A 60 Hz, 460 V, 3-phase, 6-pole wye-connected induction motor is driving a centrifugal pump at 1185 rpm. The rotational losses are 778 W and the per phase motor parameters (in ohms) referred to the stator are:

R1 = 0.2, R2 = 0.25, XM = 42, X1 = 1.2, X2 = 1.29

Determine:

(a) Slip

(b) Input line current

(c) Input power

(d) Air gap power

(e) Rotor copper loss

(f) Developed power

(g) Developed torque

(h) Load torque

(i) Motor efficiency

Answers:

(a) 0.0125

(b) 14.414 A

(c) 9796 W

(d) 9671.3 W

(e) 120.89 W

(f) 9550.41 W

(g) 76.96 Nm

(h) 70.69 Nm

(i) 89.55 %
