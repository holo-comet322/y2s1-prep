Question 1.

A 60 Hz, 460 V, 3-phase, 4-pole wye-connected induction motor has negligible rotational losses. The per phase motor parameters (in ohms) referred to the stator are: 

R1 = 0.58, R2 = 0.077, XM = 18, X1 = 0.32, X2 = 0.386

(a) If the slip is 3.5 %, determine the output power.

(b) Find the maximum torque of the motor, the corresponding slip and the rotor speed at maximum torque.

(c) Find the starting torque.

Answers:

(a) 53.268 kW

(b) 368 Nm, 0.0846, 1648 rpm

(c) 90.45 Nm
