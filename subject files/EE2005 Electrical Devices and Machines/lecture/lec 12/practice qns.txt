Question 1.

A single-phase, 20 kVA, 2200/220 V two-winding transformer is connected as a 2420/220 V autotransformer.

(a) Determine the maximum rated output power of the autotransformer.

(b) Calculate the power transferred by induction, and the power delivered by conduction.

Answers:

(a) 22 kVA

(b) 20 kVA and 2 kVA
