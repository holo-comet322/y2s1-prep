Question 1.

Suppose three identical units of the single-phase transformer, each single-phase transformer rated at 20 kVA, 2200/220 V, are to be connected to form a three-phase Δ/Y transformer bank. The equivalent impedance of each single-phase transformer referred to its low-voltage side is (0.04 + j0.12) Ω.

(a) Determine the ratings of the three-phase transformer bank.

(b) Determine the input current and the input voltage of the three-phase transformer bank when it delivers full load at 0.85 lagging power factor at rated voltage. Ignore the no-load current.

Answers:

(a) 60 kVA, 2200 V, 381.05 V

(b) 15.74 A and 2289.80 V
