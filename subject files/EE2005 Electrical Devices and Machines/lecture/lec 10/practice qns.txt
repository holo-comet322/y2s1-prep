Question 1.

A 50 kVA, 1200/120 V, 50 Hz, single-phase transformer has the following parameters:

   R1 = 0.45 Ω
 X1 = 2.2 Ω


   R2 = 0.0045 Ω
 X2 = 0.022 Ω


   Rc1 = 10 kΩ
 Xm1 = 8 kΩ

   

A load of 30 kW at 0.75 lagging power factor is delivered at the low-voltage terminals at rated voltage. Using the approximate equivalent circuit referred to the low-voltage side, calculate:

(a) The input voltage,

(b) The voltage regulation, and

(c) The efficiency of the transformer at the given load condition.

Answers:

(a) 1322.6 V

(b) 10.22%

(c) 96.23%
