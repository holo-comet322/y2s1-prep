Question 1.

The coil of a magnetic circuit has a resistance of 5 Ω. If the coil is connected to a 10 V dc source, determine the current in the coil. 

Answer: 2 A



Question 2.

The electromagnetic relay is used in an aircraft. The coil has a resistance of 4 Ω and an inductance of 0.04 H. Find the current in the coil if the coil is supplied with a 120 V, 400 Hz ac source.  

Answer: 1.193 A
