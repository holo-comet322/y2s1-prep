A slow, from-scratch walkthrough of Weeks 1–13, built from your lecture slides and tutorials — with things to write in your own notes, and (where it fits) how to build the same logic in Minecraft redstone. Plus every question from all 7 past exam papers, fully solved.

How to use this

Each week page has four parts, always in this order:

  1. Slides, slowly — the lecture content broken into small steps, with plain-English explanations, formulas explained variable-by-variable, and analogies.
  2. Tutorial, linked back — every tutorial question for that week, tagged with exactly which slide concept it's testing, and worked through step by step.
  3. Write this down — a short list of the specific facts/definitions/formulas worth copying into your own notebook (writing it yourself, even if it's already here, is what makes it stick).
  4. Redstone build — where the topic maps onto something buildable in Minecraft, a concrete build guide translating the circuit into redstone components.

Go at whatever pace you need — re-read a section, or come back to the tutorial after re-reading the slides. There's no rush; the goal this time is understanding the "why", not just memorising the "what".
