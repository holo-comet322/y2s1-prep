Exam Focus — What the IE2104 Finals Really Test

Seven past papers (2022/23 S2 – 2025/26 S2) analysed by topic and marks, plus a post-mortem of AY25/26 S2

📡 What the finals test
  • The paper is a template. From 2023-24 S1 onwards every paper follows (almost) the same 4-question skeleton: Q1 = number systems + Boolean/K-map minimisation + a CMOS gate; Q2 = three function-realisation parts with MUX / decoder / PLA / full adder; Q3 = sequential-circuit analysis or design (+ latches or timing); Q4 = counters/registers + timing or memory. Each question is worth 25 marks. (The oldest paper, 2022-23 S2, scrambles the order but has the same content.)
  • Function realisation (Q2) is the largest single block: 152 of 700 marks (22%), in all 7 papers, with the same three devices tested every time. FSM + timing together are another 182 marks.
  • For Quiz 2 (weeks 4–8, 10 MCQs): expect Boolean algebra identities and minterm/maxterm conversions (Wk 4), K-map groups, SOP/POS and don’t-cares (Wk 5), what a decoder/MUX/PLA does (Wk 6), comparator/adder behaviour (Wk 7) and latch/flip-flop characteristic tables and timing (Wk 8). 🩺 Post-mortem: AY2025-26 Semester 2 (the paper you failed)

Q1 and Q2 were 25 marks each of “recipe” questions you can fully rehearse; Q3(a) alone was 20 marks (a full Mealy/Moore analysis: excitation functions → state table → diagram), and Q4 was a standard 3-bit synchronous counter. Nothing in the paper was a surprise topic: every part has near-identical siblings in the earlier six papers.

⚠ Where marks were most at risk (and how to protect them)
  • Q3(a), 20 marks, a single multi-step analysis. Mistakes early (wrong excitation equations) wreck all four sub-parts. Practise the fixed recipe in Wk 9/Wk 10: read the circuit → write D and T inputs → fill the state table with Q+ (using Q+ = D and Q+ = T⊕Q) → state diagram. Do at least 4 of the earlier FSM-analysis parts timed.
  • Q4(c), 10 marks, design from state table. Same template as the earlier synchronous-counter design questions (2024-25 S1 Q4(a), 2025-26 S1 Q4(a)): next-state K-maps → D/T inputs → circuit. Drill Wk 12 until the K-maps take ~3 minutes each.
  • Q1(b), 10 marks, minimise and prove equivalence. Two parts: K-map SOP and POS, then Boolean-algebra proof that they are equal. The proof is a separately-scored step, so do not skip it (see the Wk 4 theorems).
  • Q2(b), 9 marks, full adder with a PLA + XOR. A full adder is S = A⊕B⊕Ci, Co = AB + Ci(A⊕B). Appeared in each of the last three papers (decoder, MUX, PLA, composite). Learn it as one block.

🎯 What to focus on, in order

  1. Function realisation with MUX / decoder / PLA / full adder (152 marks): Wk 6. Same three recipes, every year.
  2. FSM analysis and design (124 marks, plus counters 90): Wk 9, 10, 11, 12.
  3. Timing: setup/hold margin calculations (58 marks), in 7/7 papers: Wk 8.
  4. CMOS gate design (64 marks), about 9 marks per paper, a near-certain gain: Wk 2.
  5. K-map SOP/POS with don’t-cares + number systems (121 marks): Wk 5, Wk 1.
  6. Memory (address/data lines, DRAM refresh): a quick 4–5 marks in 4 of 7 papers: Wk 13.
