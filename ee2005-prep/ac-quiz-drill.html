📘 Speed & slip

Q1

Synchronous speed of an 8-pole, 60 Hz machine (rpm)?

Reveal answer
Answer: 900 rpm

ns = 120f/p = 120(60)/8.

Q2

4-pole, 50 Hz motor running at 1470 rpm. Slip?

Reveal answer
Answer: 0.02

s = (1500−1470)/1500 = 0.02.

Q3

Slip 0.04 on a 60 Hz supply. Rotor induced frequency (Hz)?

Reveal answer
Answer: 2.4 Hz

fr = sf = 0.04(60).

Q4

6-pole, 60 Hz motor at slip 3.5 %. Rotor speed (rpm)?

Reveal answer
Answer: 1158 rpm

n = ns(1−s) = 1200(0.965).

Q5

Slip and rotor frequency at standstill?

Reveal answer
Answer: s = 1, fr = f

Rotor stationary: the field sweeps past at full synchronous speed.

📘 Power flow

Q6

Pag = 10 kW, s = 0.04. Rotor copper loss?

Reveal answer
Answer: 400 W

Pcu2 = sPag.

Q7

Pag = 20 kW, s = 0.05. Power developed?

Reveal answer
Answer: 19000 W

Pdev = (1−s)Pag.

Q8

Pcu2 = 1.5 kW at s = 0.03. Air-gap power?

Reveal answer
Answer: 50000 W

Pag = Pcu2/s.

Q9

4-pole, 50 Hz motor with Pag = 31.4 kW. Developed torque?

Reveal answer
Answer: 199.9 N·m

Tdev = Pag/ωs, ωs = 2π(1500)/60 = 157.08.

Q10

Pout = 15 kW at 1440 rpm. Load torque?

Reveal answer
Answer: 99.5 N·m

T = Pout/ωm, ωm = 150.80 rad/s.

Q11

440 V line, 30 A, pf 0.8 lagging. Input power?

Reveal answer
Answer: 18290 W

Pin = √3VLILcosφ.

Q12

I = 30 A, R1 = 0.2 Ω/phase. Stator copper loss?

Reveal answer
Answer: 540 W

Pcu1 = 3I²R1 (three phases).

Q13

Pout = 28 700 W, Pin = 32 389 W. Efficiency?

Reveal answer
Answer: 88.6 %

η = Pout/Pin.

Q14

Why is the quantity R2(1−s)/s special? Evaluate for R2 = 0.2 Ω, s = 0.04.

Reveal answer
Answer: 4.8 Ω

It is the ‘load resistor’ in the equivalent circuit, representing the power converted to mechanical: Pdev = 3I2²R2(1−s)/s.

📘 Equivalent circuit

Q15

Phase voltage of a 3300 V (line) Y-connected motor?

Reveal answer
Answer: 1905.3 V

Vφ = VL/√3.

Q16

R2 = 0.2 Ω at s = 0.04. What is R2/s?

Reveal answer
Answer: 5.0 Ω

Rotor branch resistance in the per-phase circuit.

Q17

Input impedance formula of the per-phase circuit?

Reveal answer
Answer: Zin = R1+jX1 + jXM ∥ (R2/s + jX2)

Stator branch in series with the parallel of magnetising and rotor branches.

📘 Thevenin

Q18

Vth and Zth expressions?

Reveal answer
Answer: Vth = VφXM/|R1+j(X1+XM)|; Zth = jXM(R1+jX1)/(R1+j(X1+XM))

Voltage divider and parallel impedance seen from the rotor branch.

Q19

Formula for smax and Tmax?

Reveal answer
Answer: smax = R2/√(Rth²+(Xth+X2)²); Tmax = 3Vth²/(2ωs[Rth+√(Rth²+(Xth+X2)²)])

From maximum power transfer to the R2/s resistor.

Q20

True or false: raising R2 increases the maximum torque.

Reveal answer
Answer: False

Tmax is independent of R2; only smax (∝ R2) shifts.

Q21

Supply voltage falls 10 %. Maximum torque becomes what fraction of before?

Reveal answer
Answer: 0.81

Tmax ∝ V² = 0.9².

📘 R1=X1=0 shortcuts

Q22

R1 = X1 = 0, R2 = 0.3 Ω, X2 = 1.5 Ω. Slip at maximum torque?

Reveal answer
Answer: 0.20

smax = R2/X2.

Q23

smax = 0.2. What fraction of Tmax is developed at s = 0.05?

Reveal answer
Answer: 0.4706

T/Tmax = 2smaxs/(smax²+s²) = 0.02/0.0425.

Q24

R1 = X1 = 0, R2 = 0.4 Ω, X2 = 1.6 Ω. External rotor resistance for maximum starting torque?

Reveal answer
Answer: 1.2 Ω

Need R2+Rext = X2 ⇒ 1.6 − 0.4.

Q25

R1 = X1 = 0, R2 = 2 Ω, X2 = 4 Ω. By what factor does the starting torque change if Rext = 2 Ω is added?

Reveal answer
Answer: 1.25

Tst ∝ R/(R²+X2²): new 4/32 = 0.125, old 2/20 = 0.1.

Q26

R2 is tripled and slip is tripled. What happens to torque and rotor copper loss?

Reveal answer
Answer: Torque unchanged; Pcu2 × 3

R2/s is unchanged so I2 and T are unchanged; Pcu2 = sPag triples.

📘 Tests

Q27

DC test on a Y-connected motor: 15 V, 10 A. R1?

Reveal answer
Answer: 0.75 Ω

R1 = Vdc/(2Idc).

Q28

Locked-rotor test: 900 W, 20 A (per line). Per-phase R1+R2?

Reveal answer
Answer: 0.75 Ω

RLR = P/(3I²).

Q29

Locked-rotor: 60 V line, 20 A. Per-phase |ZLR|?

Reveal answer
Answer: 1.732 Ω

(VL/√3)/I.

Q30

From RLR = 0.75 Ω, ZLR = 1.732 Ω: X1 = X2 = ?

Reveal answer
Answer: 0.781 Ω

XLR = √(Z²−R²) = 1.561; each leakage reactance is half.
