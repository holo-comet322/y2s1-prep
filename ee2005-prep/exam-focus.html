Exam Focus — What the EE2005 Finals Really Test

Six past papers (AY2023/24 – AY2025/26) by question and topic, plus the induction-motor quiz

📡 What the EE2005 finals test
  • Correction: I earlier said the finals never test machines. Each EE2005 final has four questions of 25 marks: Q1 magnetic circuits (+ force), Q2 transformers, Q3 induction motors, Q4 DC machines. The previous exam page only covered Q1–Q2, so half of the paper was missing.
  • For the quiz (AC machines only): Question 3 is exactly that material. Go through AC Machines (concepts 1–7) and the AC Quiz Drill. Since the quiz is short-answer formula questions, memorise the formula sheet at the bottom of the AC Machines page.
  • No AC-machine lecture slides exist in your folder; the tutorials T8–T10 and past papers cover only induction motors. If you were taught synchronous machines too, upload those slides.

🧭 The Q3 template, in order of likelihood

  1. Equivalent circuit at a given slip (5/6 papers): find Zin, I, Pin, Pag, Pdev, T, Pout, η.
  2. Power flow arithmetic (4/6): given T, speed and losses, climb the power chain with Pcu2 = sPag.
  3. Thevenin smax / Tmax (4/6): Zth, Vth, then the two max-torque formulas.
  4. R1 = X1 = 0 ratio tricks (4/6, 6–7 marks each, no voltage needed).
  5. Extra rotor resistance for maximum starting torque or a target torque (4/6).
  6. Tests (2/6): DC test R1 = V/(2I), locked-rotor R and X.

🔋 Question 4: DC machines (final only)

Not in the quiz, but a full 25 marks in every final. Each paper asks (a) a shunt motor (15–18 marks: torque, no-load/rotational loss, new speed with extra field or armature resistance, linear magnetisation) and (b) a separately-excited generator (7–10 marks: efficiency, field resistance for a terminal voltage). Concept page: DC Machines; all 11 parts solved in By Topic.
