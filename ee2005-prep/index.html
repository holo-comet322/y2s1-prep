How this is organised

Every week page now interleaves the two: a small concept (a formula, what it calculates, when to use it, an analogy) is immediately followed by the specific tutorial question that tests just that concept, with a "try it yourself first" reveal before the worked answer — instead of reading the whole lecture, then doing the whole tutorial afterward. The idea is to catch you right when a concept is fresh, rather than several pages later.

Each week still ends with Write This Down (a short list worth copying into your own notebook) and, where a picture genuinely helps, Visualize It. Every explanation carries a small tag next to it, like Lec 5 · p.8 — click it to jump straight to that exact page of the source lecture PDF, so you can always check an explanation against the original slide it's drawn from.

Prefer the old single-read layout (all the lecture content first, then all the tutorial questions after)? Every week page links to a "Classic layout" version — look for the note just under the page title.
