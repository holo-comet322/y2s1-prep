IE2106 Engineering Mathematics 1 — Probability & Statistics Prep

A slow, from-scratch walkthrough of Weeks 1–13, built from Dr Patricia Wong's lecture slides and tutorials — with things to write in your own notes, and diagrams to help you actually see what's going on.

How to use this

Each week page follows the same shape:

  1. Slides, slowly — the lecture content broken into small steps, with plain-English explanations, formulas explained variable-by-variable, and real-world examples.
  2. Tutorial, linked back — every tutorial question for that week, tagged with exactly which slide concept it's testing, and worked through step by step.
  3. Write this down — a short list of the specific facts/definitions/formulas worth copying into your own notebook.
  4. Visualize it — this subject is more about seeing the structure of a problem than building anything physical, so instead of a Minecraft redstone build (like the IE2104 site), each week gets small diagrams — Venn diagrams, probability trees, distribution curves — built to make the abstract idea concrete.

Go at whatever pace you need. The goal is understanding the "why" behind each formula, not just memorising the formula itself.
